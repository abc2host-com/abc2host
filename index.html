Questions

Frequently Asked

How is delivery timed for each package? +

BrandCode 360™ typically runs 5–7 weeks depending on print production and revision rounds. TechCare Code™ retainers begin the month after onboarding is complete.

How does billing work — SAR or EGP? +

You're billed in EGP. One-time packages are invoiced in two milestones (deposit + delivery); retainers are billed monthly in advance. Ad spend for paid media is always billed separately, direct to the platform.

Why a static website instead of a CMS? +

Our static/custom builds are hand-built, not run on a heavy CMS — near-instant load times, no plugins to patch, lower hosting costs, and a cleaner Core Web Vitals score. It's built for a corporate presence that states who you are, not a blog you'll update daily.

Can you work white-label for our agency? +

Yes. We regularly sit behind other agencies' and marketing departments' brands as an overflow production partner or fractional Ad-Ops/Creative lead — delivery, files, and communication under NDA and your name. Terms and pricing are scoped to your volume, not the fixed packages above.

Is there a minimum term on retainers? +

We ask for a 3-month minimum on TechCare Code™, since paid media and content need that long to gather enough data to optimize properly. After that, retainers run month-to-month.

Which regions do you cover? +

We deliver directly across Egypt, Kuwait, Saudi Arabia, and the UAE, and run campaigns across the wider MENA/GCC region. If your city isn't listed on the contact form, tell us where you're based and we'll confirm coverage on the call.
